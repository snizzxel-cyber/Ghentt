Walk inside

All rooms are furnished and have a door. Enter room keeps you in one room. Walk freely lets you walk through the whole building: doors open as you come close, walls and furniture block you, the level buttons work like a lift (or R and F), the map shows where you are (click it to jump) and a sign appears in every room. W A S D or arrow keys to move, drag to look, Esc to leave. Rooms behind a wall without an opening (some service rooms) can be visited with N and P or the list.
