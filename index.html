Ground floor · ±0.00 m

The nave stays public

No walls in the nave at street level. A 208 m² reading hall on the original tiled floor, open to the whole neighbourhood, with a café in the old choir.

Level +1 · 2.90 m

Eighteen rooms to focus

Fourteen study rooms sit on the old aisle galleries and look through the arcade into the reading hall. Four more are on the ground floor.

Levels +2 and +3 · 5.60 and 9.00 m

Home is above the books

Twenty student rooms rest on the arcade arches. On level +2 every room reaches out under the old aisle roof into an alcove with its own roof window, about 19 m² in all.

Level +4 · 12.30 m

Dinner under the ridge

No bedrooms in the attic. Instead one 132 m² kitchen for all residents, a terrace cut into the red tile roof, and 67 m² more on top of the café building.

Every level

Zero ramps, one lift

Every new floor meets the church at exactly the same height: 2.90, 5.60, 9.00 and 12.30 m. One lift in the glazed wing stops on all five levels.

Concept · section through the nave

Five levels, from public to private

The higher you go, the more private the building becomes. The ground floor belongs to everyone, the study rooms to the city’s students, the bedrooms to the residents, and the kitchen under the ridge is the shared living room.

    The church stays one space

    Nothing is built into the nave at ground level. The tiled floor stays visible, the organ gallery is kept, and the last bay stays open up to the roof, so the organ and the east wall remain visible from the reading hall.

    Everything new stands at the west end

    Café, bathrooms, lift, stair and bicycle store sit in two new buildings and a glazed wing next to the choir. From Voskenslaan the church looks almost as it does today; only dormers and the small roof terrace are new.

    Levels without ramps

    The floors of the new building sit at 2.90, 5.60, 9.00 and 12.30 m, exactly level with the church floors, so the three bridges through the west gable are flat. The lift in the glazed wing stops on all five levels; only the study rooms on level +1 are reached by stair.
